Our Story

Beedy Worth Collections is a growing fashion and production brand with a history dating back to 2009. Since our establishment, we have remained committed to developing our craft, refining our products, and building a brand that reflects dedication, creativity, and quality workmanship.

Although unforeseen circumstances have, at various points, limited our ability to expand our visibility and establish a stronger global presence, they have never diminished our ambition or commitment to growth. Over the years, we have continued to build valuable experience, nurture professional relationships, and strengthen the foundation upon which our brand stands today.

At the heart of Beedy Worth Collections is our founder and Chief Executive Officer, Madam Bridget Otu, whose dedication, consistency, and forward-thinking vision continue to drive the brand towards greater opportunities. Her commitment to the business reflects our belief that lasting success is built through patience, continuous improvement, and an unwavering determination to deliver value to our customers.

Today, we are embracing a new chapter in our journey. Having spent years developing our craft and contributing to the fashion industry, we are ready to bring our work into greater public view, connect with a wider audience, and establish a stronger presence in the fashion marketplace.

Our Experience and Industry Relationships

Our journey has also been shaped by valuable collaborations and professional relationships. For approximately ten years, Beedy Worth Collections has worked alongside Busayo Olupona, founder and CEO of the Busayo brand, contributing to a working relationship that has connected our production experience with a broader appreciation for African-inspired fashion.

Through this collaboration, our products have reached customers across different locations, including the United States, where the Busayo brand has helped bring African fashion and creative expression to a wider audience.

The Busayo brand has also been associated with the wardrobes and editorial appearances of notable public figures, including Madonna, Lupita Nyong'o, Keke Palmer, Leslie Odom Jr., and Wunmi Mosaku, among others.

This connection forms part of our professional journey and reflects the wider fashion environment in which our work has developed. It is also a reminder that meaningful contributions to the fashion industry are not always made in the spotlight. Sometimes, the hands behind the scenes play an equally important role in bringing creative ideas to life.

For Beedy Worth Collections, this experience is part of our foundation as we work towards building greater brand recognition, strengthening our identity, and introducing our craftsmanship to an even broader audience.

Our Philosophy: Personalisation and Customer Satisfaction

At Beedy Worth Collections, we believe that fashion should be more than the purchase of a finished product. It should be an experience shaped around individual preferences, comfort, expectations, and personal style.

For this reason, our sales and ordering process places strong emphasis on direct communication between our customers and our team. Rather than relying exclusively on automated online orders and payments, we encourage customers to engage with us personally so that we can understand their needs, discuss their preferred designs, clarify specifications, and establish the details required for production.

This approach allows us to pay closer attention to the unique requirements of every order, particularly when dealing with customized clothing, intricate designs, and specific fitting preferences.

Our objective is to minimize avoidable misunderstandings, reduce the likelihood of unnecessary alterations, and ensure that production is guided by a clear understanding of the customer's expectations before work begins.

We value open communication, transparency, and mutual understanding because we believe that a successful business relationship extends beyond a single transaction. Our aim is to build lasting relationships with customers through attentive service, thoughtful craftsmanship, and a genuine commitment to their satisfaction.

Understanding Our Catalogue and Pricing

Our product catalogue is designed to introduce customers to our available designs, creative possibilities, and production options. However, it is important to understand that the prices displayed on our product cards are starting prices rather than fixed final prices.

These prices provide a reference point for discussions between the customer and Beedy Worth Collections. Depending on the selected design, fabric, materials, level of customisation, fitting requirements, and other production considerations, the final price may be negotiated and agreed upon before an order is confirmed.

We encourage customers to communicate their preferred budget and design expectations during the consultation process. This helps us determine the most suitable production approach and establish a mutually agreed price before proceeding.

The images displayed in our catalogue should also be viewed primarily as design references and representations of the styles we offer. The appearance of a product in the catalogue does not necessarily mean that the exact item is already manufactured and available for immediate collection or dispatch.

At Beedy Worth Collections, production is guided by the specifications agreed upon with each customer. Before manufacturing begins, we review the customer's preferred fit, design details, comfort requirements, and other relevant preferences.

Our catalogue's availability indicator provides an estimate of the production time associated with each product. This allows customers to make informed decisions and understand the expected waiting period before placing an order.

Our Production Timeline and Availability Guide

We understand that every design comes with its own production demands. Simple designs may require less preparation, while highly customized garments and intricate styles may require additional time, precision, and attention to detail.

To help customers understand our estimated production schedules, we use the following availability guide:

Availability indicator and estimated production time
Availability IndicatorEstimated Production Time
100% Availability5–10 working days
70% Availability10–13 working days
50% Availability15–18 working days
30% Availability20–25 working days
10% Availability25–30 working days

These indicators communicate the estimated time required to prepare and produce a selected item, with the applicable timeframe depending on the product's production requirements and the availability status displayed.

Please note that these periods are expressed in working days, not calendar days. The actual production schedule may also depend on the timely confirmation of design specifications, fabric and material availability, and other relevant order requirements.

While some production timelines may appear lengthy, we consider adequate preparation and execution essential to achieving the intended result. Allowing sufficient time for customized and complex designs helps us maintain attention to detail, follow agreed specifications, and reduce avoidable production errors and alterations.

Our priority is not simply to complete an order quickly, but to approach every confirmed order with the care and precision it deserves.
